VERDICT: ALIGNMENT IS NOT A REASON TO REPACK

Every per-expert stride is a whole number of sectors, so expert e and
expert e+1 sit at the same sub-sector phase and never share a sector. The
constant base-offset slop shown above is absorbed by reading the enclosing
aligned window and returning buf + slop, at a cost of at most one sector
per expert.

An unbuffered (FILE_FLAG_NO_BUFFERING) reader can address experts IN PLACE
inside the original GGUF shards.

This settles alignment only. The other argument for a repack is CONTIGUITY
(gate/up/down are three separate tensors, so one expert costs three seeks).
That was measured separately by src/expert_read_bench.c and found to be
worth about 5% at queue depth >= 2 - not worth a 76 GB write. See
docs/MEASURED_GROUND_TRUTH.md section 10.1.
