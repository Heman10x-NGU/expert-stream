HOW TO READ THIS
  If Belady is far above LRU, a lookahead-driven eviction policy (N-2) is worth
  building. If they are close, the OS page cache is already near-optimal for this
  access pattern and N-2 should be cut - the win would have to come from reading
  fewer bytes (N-3 expert skipping, smaller quant), not from smarter eviction.
