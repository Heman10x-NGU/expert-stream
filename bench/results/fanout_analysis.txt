==============================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS
==============================================================================
  The 'learned L3-42' column is the one that matters. If hedging 2 ways costs
  well under 12.00 experts/layer, routing is prefix-dominated and Saguaro-style
  fan-out transfers to an SSD-bound engine. If it sits near the 'random' column,
  it does not, and the idea dies here rather than after a week of C.

  Compare against 'seq. real tokens'. Batching over N REAL consecutive tokens
  gets its union for free, with no guessing and no wasted reads. Fan-out has to
  beat that to be worth any complexity at all.

  Layers 0-2 are frozen hash routing on the token id. Their column is expected to
  track 'random' closely. That is the design of the model, not a result.
