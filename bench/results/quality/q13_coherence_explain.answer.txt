> Explain in exactly three sentences why a solid state drive is faster than a spinning hard disk.
A solid state drive (SSD) stores data on non-volatile flash memory chips, which are accessed electronically with no moving parts, eliminating the mechanical latency of a spinning platter. In contrast, a hard disk drive (HDD) must physically rotate its disk and move an actuator arm to locate data, adding milliseconds of delay for every read or write operation. Because SSDs bypass this physical movement, they can transfer data at near-instantaneous speeds, dramatically reducing boot times, file access, and overall system responsiveness.
